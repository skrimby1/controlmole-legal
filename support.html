Support

Stuck, found a bug, or want to tell Mole something? Email software@magiskmg.com and a real person will answer, usually within two working days. It helps to say whether you're on Android or iPhone, and which phone you have.

Common questions

Mole stopped blocking my apps on iPhone. Check that ControlMole still has Screen Time access in Settings > Screen Time > Apps with Screen Time Access. If it was switched off, switch it back on there.

Mole stopped counting on Android. Check that Usage access (and the Mole watch, if you use it) is still on for ControlMole in Android's settings. Some phones switch apps off to save battery; let ControlMole run in the background.

How do I get Premium back on a new phone? Open Mole Premium in ControlMole and tap Restore, signed in to the same Apple or Google account you bought it with.

How do I cancel my subscription? On iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. On Android, open Google Play, tap your profile, then Payments & subscriptions. Premium stays on until the end of the period you've paid for.

How do I delete ControlMole with Mole Lock on? Tap "Let me leave" under Mole Lock in ControlMole and wait out the 24 hours; then you get a 15-minute window. On iPhone you can also switch off ControlMole's Screen Time access in Settings, which ends Mole Lock straight away.
